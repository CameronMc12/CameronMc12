# DESIGN.md — the profile's grammar

Everything a human sees on `github.com/CameronMc12` is generated by the scripts
in `scripts/`. This file is the contract they are written against. Change a
token here and re-run `python3 scripts/render_all.py`; never hardcode a colour
in a renderer.

## 0. How to use this file

Read sections 1–5 before touching a renderer. Section 10 is the running record
of what Cameron has ruled on — it wins over anything else in this file.

## 1. Platform, locale, voice

Desktop-first. Measured live: GitHub's profile README column is **846px** at a
1512px viewport and **766px** at 1200px. Assets are authored at **880** and are
therefore always scaled down — so `shoot.py` grades them at 846, not at 1:1.
Anything that only reads at full size does not read. The wordmark's glyph size
was raised from 0.86 to 0.94 of the cell for exactly this reason.

Locale en-ZA. Voice is flat and factual — the panels state numbers, they do not
sell.

## 2. Direction

**Float instrument panel.** The grammar from Cameron's Float finance OS, applied
to a profile: panels with labelled bars, near-monochrome surfaces, tabular
numbers, one accent held back for the number that matters.

Explicitly NOT the green-terminal look that the reference profile
(`AVIVASHISHTA29/AVIVASHISHTA29`) and most animated READMEs use — traffic-light
title bars, `$ whoami` prompts, GitHub-green heatmaps. That aesthetic is the
most-cloned on the platform.

## 3. Tokens

All in `scripts/theme.py`. Two themes, emitted as a pair and selected by the
README's `<picture media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">`.

| Role | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#0A0A0B` | `#F6F6F3` |
| panel | `#131315` | `#FFFFFF` |
| inset | `#1B1B1F` | `#F2F2EE` |
| border | `#242428` | `#E3E3DE` |
| text | `#EDEDEF` | `#16161A` |
| muted | `#8A8A93` | `#6C6C74` |
| dim | `#56565E` | `#9C9CA4` |
| **accent** | `#E4F222` | `#6E7D00` |

The accent is lime, and on white it reads as deep olive because on a light
ground "more" has to mean darker. The heatmap ramp runs the same hue in both
directions for the same reason.

Type is one family: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`.
Radius 14 on panels, 2.5 on heatmap cells. Panel bar 38px, padding 18px.

## 4. Panel anatomy

```
┌ LABEL ─────────────────────────── meta ┐   38px bar, 10px/600 caps, 1.4 tracking
│                                        │   content, 18px padding
├────────────────────────────────────────┤   footer rule
│ 4,292 contributions …      best day 132│   40–44px footer strip
└────────────────────────────────────────┘   1px border, radius 14
```

Panels never sit on hairlines-on-void. Every section is a card.

## 5. Art panels vs data panels

The two **art** panels (rain, wordmark) keep a near-black body in BOTH themes;
only their outer frame adapts. The two **data** panels (system, contributions)
follow the reader's theme completely.

This is not laziness. Digital rain and ASCII shading are photographs: the whole
read depends on sparse marks glowing against black, and on white the same marks
are a ghost that no amount of ink opacity rescues — measured, twice, before this
rule was written. A photograph does not invert for light mode either.

## 7. Motion

Reveals play **once and freeze** (`animation-fill-mode: both`, no
`repeatCount`). The only two things that loop are the rain and the wordmark's
rock, because both are textures rather than read-outs. A graph that pulses
forever is a screensaver.

## 9. Colour discipline

Contrast is spent, not sprayed. Per panel, at most ONE thing gets the accent:

- heatmap → the total contributions figure, and the top of the cell ramp
- system card → the current streak, and the largest language segment
- wordmark → nothing. Every attempt to accent a "brightest" tier painted the
  whole word lime, because the front caps of an extrusion all shade within a
  couple of ramp steps of each other.
- rain → nothing lime. Phosphor green is its own thing, see §10.

## 10. Rulings

- **2026-08-27 — Matrix rain in film phosphor green, not the lime accent.**
  Cameron asked for the movie look. Green appears exactly once on the page, in
  one panel, as a texture. A lime variant exists behind
  `render_matrix.py --palette lime` and is not shipped.
- **2026-08-27 — Direction is Float instrument panel**, chosen over the
  reference's terminal-green and over a blueprint/schematic option.
- **2026-08-27 — Private repo NAMES never appear in this repo.** They are client
  work. Aggregate language bytes are fine; the "clients" line on the card is
  hand-written in `data/identity.json` and is Cameron's call, not the API's.
